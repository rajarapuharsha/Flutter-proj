# VEDIC Portal Submission Summary

**Project title:** BMI & Daily Calorie Calculator — Healthy Living and SDG 3

**Abstract:** A responsive Flutter and Dart educational application that demonstrates BMI calculation and adult daily calorie estimates. It uses input validation, adult BMI categories, Mifflin-St Jeor BMR equations, activity multipliers, and selectable calorie goals. The app includes clear limitations and a safe under-18 experience.

**Problem statement:** Learners need an accessible demonstration of how common BMI and energy estimates are calculated, together with an explanation that these values are not diagnoses or individual prescriptions.

**Objectives:** Calculate BMI; classify adult screening ranges; estimate adult BMR and activity-adjusted maintenance calories; demonstrate goal adjustments, validation, responsive UI, and SDG 3 awareness.

**Key features:** Offline calculations, responsive Material 3 interface, validation and reset, BMI indicator, estimates and general advice, SDG 3 information, privacy and health disclaimer, automated tests authored.

**Technology stack:** Flutter, Dart, Material 3, Flutter test.

**SDG alignment:** SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-Being. Educational awareness of body measurements, estimated energy needs, balanced nutrition, and movement. No measured social impact is claimed.

**Expected social impact:** Potentially support awareness and classroom discussion; this expected benefit has not been measured.
